On 2026-09-27 the owner said Kaze Emanuar (The Missing Link's author) gave them permission to port ML into Ship of Harkinian.

**Why it matters:** it covers ML's own work: the ported C code in the engine fork, ML's original assets and text. It does not cover Nintendo's data. ROMs, `oot.o2r`, and the OoT-derived assets inside the mod archive stay Nintendo's. So the [[no-copyrighted-data-in-git]] rules and the "players build the mod from their own ROM before a public release" question still stand.

**How to apply:** don't treat ML-authored code or content as a legal blocker any more. Keep crediting Kaze and team. When public-release questions come up, separate ML's parts (permitted) from Nintendo's parts (never distributed).

**Owner, 2026-10-19:** "can use ML as long as people still need to go download ML from his site when they use this." Packages may carry small pieces of ML's own work, like builder-3's title-card banner. What must hold is that players still get ML v2.0 (the patch) from Kaze's page ([[ml-official-site]]): never ship the patch, a patched ROM, or enough of ML to play without it. Nintendo's data stays out entirely. builder-3 stays up until builder-4 replaces it. Builder-4 reads the banner from the player's ROM anyway.
